## Gate arithmetic after the pilot

- **Generic-vs-baseline gate** (needs ≥4/6 pairwise wins): generic is **0/2** — it must win
  4/4 remaining to pass. Long odds; the pilot shows no signal in that direction.
- **Repository-context-lift gate** (initialized loses no more than 1 vs generic): initialized
  has **2 losses already — the lift gate is formally failed** regardless of the remaining
  four cases.

## What actually happened, mechanically

- On frozen-error-prototype, generic and initialized produced (accurate) empty reviews; the
  baseline found three **confirmed-valid** low-severity findings — including a mutation check
  (the new test passes even with the fixed block deleted) — and won on actionability. The
  rubric's "empty beats speculative" rule did not save the candidate because the baseline's
  volume was *supported*.
- On agent-guardrails-input, all three arms converged on the same core dead-hint finding;
  baseline and generic matched at 10; initialized dropped a point on selectivity.
- The profile was **fresh and used** in both initialized runs (per-claim staleness fix worked;
  agents reported it directing verification passes) — but the direction it added never
  converted into findings the other arms lacked. Source-valid profile uses: present, but no
  material finding lift.
- Candidate-mechanics observation: review-pr's strict anchoring/validation pushed both pr-kit
  arms toward conservative reporting; the confirmed-valid low-severity findings the baseline
  scored with would have needed anchoring effort the pr-kit arms did not spend.

## Verdict

Pilot recommendation: **do not proceed to the remaining four cases on this candidate.** The
lift gate is already mathematically failed and the generic gate would need a 4/4 sweep against
a baseline that has been at least as accurate and more complete. Per the pre-registered build
gate, pr-kit remains unpublished.

Known caveats, recorded rather than argued: n=2; all arms ran on the same underlying model;
single judge per case; the baseline arm is a strong general reviewer prompt rather than a
literal `/code-review` invocation (same spirit as the contract's "strongest generally
available" baseline).

If the candidate is revisited, the evidence points at one specific lever: the validation
contract suppresses supported low-severity findings (the baseline's winning margin), not
accuracy — a revision would loosen the anchoring cost for low-priority findings, then re-run
Phase-0 fresh.
